Our Story

Born from a love of the perfect cup

Steam & Bean started in 2019 as a tiny roastery in Portland, Oregon - two friends who believed coffee should be an experience, not just a caffeine delivery system.

Today we source beans from over 30 farms across six countries, roast them in small batches, and deliver the freshest possible cup to your door. Every bag is traceable back to the farmer who grew it.
