The Story

Ella Langley is a country music singer and songwriter who has been making waves in the Nashville scene with her powerful, unfiltered voice and deeply personal songwriting. Growing up in Oklahoma, she was surrounded by the sounds of classic country, rock, and gospel - influences that shaped her into the artist she is today.

Her music blends traditional country storytelling with a modern edge, drawing comparisons to artists like Kacey Musgraves, Maren Morris, and Miranda Lambert. Ella's lyrics are honest, vulnerable, and unapologetically real - whether she's singing about heartbreak, small-town life, or the freedom of the open road.

Known for her electrifying live performances, Ella has built a devoted following through relentless touring and an authentic connection with her audience. She plays guitar, writes every song herself, and brings a raw energy to the stage that leaves crowds wanting more.
