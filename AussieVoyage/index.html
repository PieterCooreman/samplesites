Iconic Destinations

Australia is home to some of the most spectacular landscapes on Earth. Here are the must-visit places.

Marine

Great Barrier Reef

The world's largest coral reef system, stretching over 2,300 km along Queensland's coast. Home to 1,500 species of fish and 400 types of coral.

Sacred

Uluru & Kata Tjuta

The sacred monolith of Uluru rises 348m from the red desert. A spiritual heartland for the Anangu people, glowing crimson at sunset.

Urban

Sydney Harbour

The iconic Opera House and Harbour Bridge frame one of the world's most beautiful natural harbours. A city where urban meets ocean.

Nature

Daintree Rainforest

The oldest surviving tropical rainforest on Earth, dating back 180 million years. Where the reef meets the rainforest in Queensland.

Wilderness

Tasmania

Australia's island state of pristine wilderness. Cradle Mountain, Lake St Clair, and the wild Southwest National Park await adventurers.

Coastal

Great Ocean Road

A 243 km stretch of coastal road along Victoria's dramatic shoreline. The Twelve Apostles limestone stacks rise from the Southern Ocean.

Unforgettable Experiences

Australia offers adventures that you'll remember for a lifetime.

Diving & Snorkeling

Swim alongside sea turtles, manta rays, and thousands of tropical fish in crystal-clear waters.

Sunset at Uluru

Watch the monolith transform from ochre to deep crimson as the sun dips below the horizon.

Wildlife Encounters

Meet kangaroos, koalas, wombats, and the unique wildlife found nowhere else on Earth.

Road Trips

Drive the legendary Outback highways, from Perth to Adelaide or along the coastal routes.

Astrotourism

Experience the Southern Hemisphere's clearest night skies in the Outback and Warrumbungle.

Hiking Trails

Walk the Overland Track, Larapinta Trail, or the coastal paths of the Blue Mountains.

Food & Wine

Explore the Barossa Valley, Margaret River, and Melbourne's world-class café culture.

Cultural Immersion

Connect with Aboriginal communities and learn about 65,000 years of living culture.

Suggested 10-Day Itinerary

A curated journey through Australia's highlights, from the tropical north to the ancient outback.

Days 1–3 - Sydney

Opera House, Harbour Bridge climb, Bondi to Coogee coastal walk, and a day trip to the Blue Mountains.

Days 4–5 - Cairns & Great Barrier Reef

Fly north to tropical Queensland. Snorkel the reef, explore Daintree Rainforest, and cruise the Fitzroy River.

Days 6–7 - Uluru

Journey to the Red Centre. Sunrise at Uluru, visit Kata Tjuta, and experience an Aboriginal cultural tour.

Days 8–9 - Melbourne & Great Ocean Road

Drive the iconic coastal road, see the Twelve Apostles, and explore Melbourne's laneways and coffee scene.

Day 10 - Departure

A final morning in Melbourne before heading to the airport for your journey home.
