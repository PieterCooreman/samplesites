The Defender mindset

Built to go beyond the map

All-terrain DNA

From exposed rocky ridges to city kerbs, Defender’s engineering is built to absorb whatever the world throws at it.

Ready for every route

Heritage

A legacy that never stopped moving

Defender began life after the Second World War as a utilitarian workhorse. Series Land Rovers were built for farms, forests and expeditions - and in 1990 the family was officially named Defender.

Seven decades later, the name remains one of the most respected in automotive history. The latest Defender generation respects that lineage, adding modern comfort, clever connectivity and genuine capability.

  • Class-leading wading and all-terrain engineering
  • Iconic design cues from the original Series I
  • 90, 110 and 130 configurations for every journey
