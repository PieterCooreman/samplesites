Our Story

From a wobbly cart to your happy cup

Jitterbean started as a lemonade-stand-sized coffee cart with a slightly crooked sign and an dangerously optimistic owner. Today our beans are still ethically sourced, our milk is still frothed with questionable enthusiasm, and our lattes still taste like a hug from a cartoon bear.

Every cup is a tiny event. We roast in small batches, dance during grinding, and hand-stamp your cup with a smile. No boring sips allowed.
