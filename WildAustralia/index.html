Where to go

Six places that will reset your horizon

Every region of Australia plays a different song. Pick a first chapter - we'll help you connect the rest.

New South Wales

Sydney & the Coast

Harbour ferries at golden hour, the Bondi-to-Coogee cliff walk and the country's most serious coffee culture.

Northern Territory

Uluru & the Red Centre

Sunrise on a monolith older than the Himalayas, then a night sky so bright it feels like a secret.

Queensland

Great Barrier Reef

Dive the world's largest living structure, then slow down in the rainforest of the Daintree.

Victoria

Melbourne & Great Ocean Road

Laneway street art and rooftop bars, then 243 km of coastal switchbacks to the Twelve Apostles.

Tasmania

Tasmania's Wilderness

Cradle Mountain at dawn, empty beaches, whisky distilleries and the unmissable MONA in Hobart.

Western Australia

Margaret River & the West

Surf breaks, tall karri forest, cellar doors and the whitest sand you have ever walked on.

Things to do

Experiences worth crossing the planet for

Australia rewards the curious. Whether you dive, drive, hike or taste your way through it, the scale always wins.

Reef diving & snorkelling

Liveaboard trips, day boats from Cairns and easy shore snorkelling on Ningaloo Reef.

Outback road trips

Empty highways, roadhouse coffee and sunsets that last a full hour. 4WD recommended.

Wildlife encounters

Kangaroos at dawn, quokkas on Rottnest, whale season along the east coast.

First Nations journeys

Guided walks, rock art sites and storytelling with Aboriginal guides who know the land.

Coastal drives & surf

Learn to surf at Byron Bay or chase swell on the wild south-west coast.

Food, wine & coffee

Barossa shiraz, Sydney rock oysters and a flat white culture that takes itself seriously.

“The outback doesn't shout. It just waits - until the noise in your head runs out.”

Suggested route

14 days across a whole continent

A relaxed first-timer loop that mixes city, desert, reef and wilderness - with real rest days built in. Every leg is bookable as private or small-group.

Get this itinerary free
Days 1–3

Sydney

Ferry to Manly, walk the Bondi-to-Coogee cliff path, eat your way through Surry Hills.

Days 4–6

Uluru & Kata Tjuta

Sunrise at the rock, a dot-painting workshop with Anangu artists, Field of Light after dark.

Days 7–9

Cairns & the Reef

Two days on the outer reef, one full day in the Daintree rainforest and Cape Tribulation.

Days 10–12

Melbourne & the Great Ocean Road

Laneway coffee and galleries, then the Twelve Apostles right at golden hour.

Days 13–14

Tasmania

Cradle Mountain hike, a final long lunch in Hobart and MONA before the flight home.
