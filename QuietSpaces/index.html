About

A practice rooted in clarity.

Founded by Adrian Sterling in 2016, Arcline Studio is a small architecture practice based in Copenhagen. We believe that good architecture is not about excess, but about clarity - spaces that feel calm, function effortlessly, and age beautifully.

Our work spans private homes, galleries, and small cultural institutions. Each project begins with listening: to the site, the light, and the people who will inhabit it. We work closely with craftsmen and local communities to bring honest materials and refined details to life.

Process

How we bring ideas to life.

01

Listen

We begin by understanding the site, the brief, and the people who will use the space.

02

Sketch

Ideas take shape through hand drawings, models, and early material studies.

03

Refine

We test daylight, circulation, and detail until the design feels inevitable.

04

Build

We collaborate closely with craftsmen to realize the work with precision and care.
