About the studio

Architecture with a deliberately light touch.

Studio Marchetti is a Copenhagen-based practice founded by Elena Marchetti in 2016. We design residential, cultural and workspace architecture for people who value calm, clarity and connection to context.

Every project begins with listening - to the land, the light, and the people who will inhabit it. The result is architecture that feels timeless, tactile, and deeply at home in its setting.

The approach

Three principles guide every project.

Context

Each project begins by reading the landscape, climate and community, so the building belongs where it stands.

Materiality

We work with a restrained palette of stone, timber, plaster and light - materials that age gracefully and feel honest.

Light

Natural light is treated as a building material, shaping rooms that feel quiet, warm and generous throughout the day.
